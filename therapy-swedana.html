About this therapy

What is Swedana?

Swedana is a gentle herbal steam therapy. After oil massage, warm herbal steam is applied to the body to open the pores, loosen stiffness and prepare the body for deeper Panchakarma cleansing.

Benefits

Benefits of Swedana

  • ✓Eases stiffness and muscle tightness
  • ✓Relieves heaviness in the body
  • ✓Supports comfort in joints
  • ✓Opens the pores and promotes sweating
  • ✓Prepares the body for Panchakarma
  • ✓Leaves the body light and relaxed

Timing & frequency

When and how often should it be done?

Duration

15–25 minutes

How often

Usually after Abhyangam for 3–7 days, or as advised by the doctor

Best time

Morning or late morning, on a light stomach.

Best suited for

People with body stiffness, heaviness, muscle tightness or joint discomfort

The exact schedule is decided by our doctor after understanding your constitution and health.

The experience

How is it done?

  1. 1

    Oil massage

    A short oil massage is usually done first.

  2. 2

    Steam chamber

    You sit or lie comfortably with your head outside the steam, kept cool.

  3. 3

    Herbal steam

    Warm herbal steam is applied gradually while the therapist watches your comfort.

  4. 4

    Cooling down

    The steam is stopped when light sweating begins, and you rest.

  5. 5

    Wipe and rest

    The body is wiped dry and you rest before going out.

Good to know

Precautions & who should check first

  • Not suitable in pregnancy, high blood pressure, heart conditions or fever
  • Avoid if dehydrated, weak or just after a meal
  • Drink warm water afterwards and avoid cold air
  • Stop and tell the therapist if you feel dizzy

This is a traditional wellbeing practice and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.
